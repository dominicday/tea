# A Tale of Two Scripts

It was the best of times, it was the worst of times, it was the age of Python, it was the age of C++, it was the epoch of belief, it was the epoch of incredulity, it was the season of Light, it was the season of Darkness, it was the spring of hope, it was the winter of despair.

In this humble repository, we present a chronicle of two scripts, alike in dignity, in fair Tea-verona, where we lay our scene.

## The Contents of the Satchel

Herein lies the `bag`, a vessel of `Yorkshire Gold`, and the `leaf`, a simple `Yorkshire` remnant. And lo! A new arrival, the `cup`, filled with the essence of `Tea`.

## The Scripts of Many Colours

First, we have `hello.py`, a script of the Pythonic persuasion. It speaks in tongues of rainbow, painting the terminal with the vibrant hues of life itself. It seeks the truth within the files, and finding it, proclaims it to the world.

Second, we have `hello.cpp`, a compiled beast of burden, forged in the fires of `g++`. It mirrors its serpent brother, delivering the same message of hope and tea, adorned in the same spectral raiment.

## Usage, or How to Summon the Spirits

To awaken the Python spirit:
```bash
python3 hello.py
```

To conjure the C++ apparition:
```bash
g++ -o hello hello.cpp
./hello
```

Proceed with caution, dear reader, for the colors may dazzle the eye and the tea may tempt the palate.
